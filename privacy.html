Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to DreamyDiaries. Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains how the app handles your information.

1. Personal Diary Data

Your diary entries, tasks, moods and favourites are stored locally in your browser using local storage. DreamyDiaries does not upload this diary content to our servers.

2. No User Accounts

DreamyDiaries does not currently require registration or user accounts.

3. Backups

You can export your diary data as a backup file and import it later. These files may contain personal information. Keep them somewhere safe and do not share them with others.

4. Internet and Third-Party Services

The app may load fonts from Google Fonts, which can involve requests to Google's servers. If hosted online, the hosting provider may also process basic technical information such as server logs.

5. Advertising

The current version does not include advertisements. If advertising is introduced in the future, this policy will be updated to explain the relevant advertising services and data practices.

6. Children's Privacy

DreamyDiaries is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. Users should follow the applicable age requirements in their country.

7. Your Responsibility

Because diary data is stored in your browser, clearing browser data, changing devices or uninstalling the browser may cause data loss. Please export backups regularly.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as DreamyDiaries develops. Changes will be published on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, please use the contact information provided on the official DreamyDiaries website when available.
